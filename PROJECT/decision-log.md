# Elmira Sleep — Decision Log

## DEC-001 — Sleep Enquiry Qualification Architecture
**Date:** 29 September 2026
**Status:** APPROVED

### Decision
Keep the existing Sleep Enquiry journey through Consultation Done and use the native Qualify Lead transition to move the Sleep Enquiry to Qualified.

### Approved Flow
Sales Ready → Consultation Done → Qualify Lead → Qualified

### BANT Treatment
BANT is a section within Consultation and is completed as part of the consultation. BANT is therefore not a separate Sleep Enquiry status.

### Rationale
The qualification assessment is performed during the consultation rather than as a separate lifecycle stage.
